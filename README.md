## 🇬🇧 English

Builds a goal-oriented wealth plan for an individual or family: cash flow, emergency reserves, debt, protection, education and retirement goals, asset allocation, scenario stress tests and an action checklist — for a first plan, an annual review or a major life change. Jurisdiction and risk tolerance must be confirmed before any conclusion; it plans and analyses, and does not replace licensed investment, tax, insurance or legal advice. Output opens with an auditable summary of no more than five lines, and every precise figure in the body carries a source tag: primary issuer, financial data source, user-stated, derived by an explicit formula, or flagged as an analyst-set threshold. When a key input is missing it neither assumes nor refuses — it rewrites that part as a tiered table, pushes second-hand or prior-only magnitudes into assumption cards, and keeps unverified entities out of the conclusion.

### ✨ What it does

- Four baseline tables: cash flow, emergency reserves, debt, protection
- Education and retirement goals turned into tiered funding-gap tables
- Asset allocation guidance plus scenario stress testing
- Every precise figure carries its own source tag; anything unsourced is marked unverified
- Missing key inputs become a tiered table instead of an assumption or a refusal
- An auditable summary of five lines or less, followed by a concrete action checklist
